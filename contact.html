FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about custom proposals, MICE bookings, and vouchers.

How quickly will I receive a customized itinerary proposal?

Initial proposal blueprints with itemized flight, villa, and transport estimates are delivered within 24 hours of your inquiry. Complex MICE events for 100+ guests are delivered within 48 hours.

Do you manage corporate MICE events for 100+ delegates?

Yes. Our Salem India Command HQ has executed summits for up to 1,200 delegates in Dubai, Zurich, and Amalfi including charter flights, venue buyouts, and security escorts.

What payment options and deposit terms apply?

We accept wire transfers, major credit cards, and corporate purchase orders. An initial 20% refundable deposit locks in your itinerary dates and luxury fleet availability.

What is your refund policy if travel schedules change?

Date modifications can be made free of charge up to 48 hours prior to departure. If cancelled 7+ days before arrival, deposits are 100% refunded immediately.
